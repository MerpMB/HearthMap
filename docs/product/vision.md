# HearthMap — Vision

## The question

> Which areas within Chiang Rai, Chiang Mai and Phayao are structurally good
> candidates for buying land to build a long-term family home?

"Structurally good" means the area is suitable for raising a family over a
**20–30 year horizon**: low natural-hazard exposure, practical access to
utilities, roads, schools and healthcare, a peaceful residential setting, a
legally and physically buildable planning context, and reasonable (not
guaranteed) long-term value.

## What HearthMap is

- A **decision-support** system: it narrows three provinces down to roughly
  10–20 serious candidate zones that justify site visits and parcel research.
- A **screening funnel**: understand the region → eliminate unsuitable land →
  score what survives → shortlist zones → only then look at parcels/listings.
- **Explainable**: every result can answer "why does this location have this
  score?" with sources, dates, resolutions, transformations and confidence.
- **Honest about uncertainty**: coarse or missing evidence is shown as such.

## What HearthMap is not

- Not a property-listing site or a listing scraper.
- Not an investment-return optimiser. *Highest expected appreciation* ≠ *best
  family home*. Value potential is one balanced dimension, framed as a
  probabilistic signal, never a promise.
- Not a substitute for due diligence: title-deed checks (chanote vs. lesser
  rights), on-site inspection, local enquiries, and legal advice remain
  mandatory. HearthMap tells you **where to look**, not **what to buy**.
- Not a judge of communities. Safety signals are used only at the resolution
  the evidence supports and never to stigmatise villages or people.

## Guiding principles

1. **Hard exclusion before ranking.** Disqualifying conditions (e.g. severe
   flood or landslide exposure, protected/forest land) cannot be compensated by
   good schools or roads.
2. **Never invent precision.** Province-level statistics are not village facts.
3. **Missing data is not positive data.** "No recorded flood" ≠ "no flood risk".
4. **Official ≠ accurate or current.** Record age, completeness and limitations.
5. **Configuration, not dogma.** Weights and thresholds are provisional and
   tunable as the family's requirements are refined.
6. **Local-first and boring.** Files, Python and QGIS before servers and UIs.
